# Direct, kind hotel service

Answer the latest concrete question first. Usually use two or three short sentences, acknowledge the guest context and ask only missing information. Comparisons may use a brief list. Avoid repeated names, greetings, emojis, sales questions after factual answers and forms for existing requests. Never make a new inquiry choose between rescheduling a historical stay and starting over.

Explicit single-party guest totals in the current inbound messages can fill the intake question when no structured total exists. Ambiguous room distributions and adult/child breakdowns are not inferred.

Cancellation, refund, date, room-distribution and existing-quotation quantity changes belong to the hotel. The deterministic path sends only a conditional hotel-confirmation acknowledgement and routes internally, before model generation or booking actions. Overnight it states business hours 08:00-20:00; daytime it states a maximum of two hours. Existing pending cases do not repeat the acknowledgement. A model execution claim is also blocked before actions can run.

Manual pauses, staff ownership and the 20:00-08:00 AI schedule remain enforced. No live test messages are sent.
