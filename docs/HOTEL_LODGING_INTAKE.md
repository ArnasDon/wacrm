# Hotel lodging intake and reference prices

For general room/lodging inquiries, collect arrival, checkout and guest count before sending room rate banners. Ask only for missing data. A qualified stay uses the weekday or weekend sheet matching its nights (Friday/Saturday are recreational); mixed stays use the calculated estimate instead of two generic sheets. Existing item photo requests and non-room categories retain their behavior.

New requests and historical stays do not supply missing intake data for a fresh inquiry. Published price questions remain eligible after staff participation; payments, negotiated rates, booking changes and final availability stay with reception.

When sharing a lodging estimate/reference, explain that hotel availability is still pending. The owner-authorized follow-up is a maximum of two hours for daytime inquiries, or reception business hours (08:00-20:00) for night inquiries. Do not promise a two-hour response overnight. The existing overnight-only AI schedule remains in force; this change does not activate daytime automatic replies.
