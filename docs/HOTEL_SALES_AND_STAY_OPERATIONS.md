# Sales versus stay operations

The AI may continue new lodging sales, published rates, practical intake questions and public menu/hour/amenity information. Existing staff priority, manual pauses and the overnight schedule remain enforced.

Assigned room numbers and keys/access, room service incidents and live meal availability require reception or restaurant staff. Routing occurs before generation, fallbacks, forms or reservation actions, without a customer-facing AI reply. Recent arrival context is retained across assistant turns and reception handoffs; old visit details do not contaminate new sales inquiries.

Regression examples: Daris asking prices for a four-adult stay remains eligible; Maria arriving at the hotel asking for her assigned room and current dinner service routes to staff. General restaurant hours and menu requests remain informational.
