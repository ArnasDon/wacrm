## Understanding Property Terms (Buyer Education)

### Carpet Area
**Definition:** The net usable floor area inside the apartment.

**What's Included:**
- All rooms (bedrooms, living room, dining room, kitchen)
- Internal partition walls
- Usable floor space

**What's Excluded:**
- External walls
- Service shafts
- Exclusive balconies/verandahs
- Exclusive open terraces
- Common areas (lobby, corridors, lifts)

**Legal Reference:** As per India's RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Authority) definition

### Common Areas (Not in Carpet Area)
- Shared lobby
- Common corridors
- Lift shafts
- Service shafts
- Balconies and terraces (exclusive use)
